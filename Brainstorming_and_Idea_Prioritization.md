## Step 1 – Team Gathering and Problem Selection
The five-member team met on 29/09/2026 and selected the problem of *untracked personal spending and poor savings habits*. Each member proposed ideas independently before the ideas were shared and grouped.

## Step 2 – Brainstorming (Ideas by Member)
| Team Member | Ideas Proposed |
|---|---|
| Sivapriya S | Monthly budget setup with category-wise limits; overspending alerts |
| Amala Merlin A | Auto-categorisation of expenses using ML; simple expense entry with quick-add buttons |
| Anjali K | Savings goal tracker with progress bar; friendly reminders |
| Balaharish | Month-end spending prediction using past data; anomaly (unusual spend) detection |
| Bharanidharan R | Dashboard with charts; AI chat assistant for questions like "How much can I spend today?" |

## Step 3 – Grouping Ideas
- **Tracking:** quick expense entry, auto-categorisation
- **Planning:** budget limits, savings goals
- **Intelligence:** spending forecast, anomaly detection, AI suggestions
- **Experience:** dashboard, alerts, chat assistant

## Step 4 – Idea Prioritization
| Idea | Impact | Effort | Priority |

| Budget limits and overspending alerts | High | Low | **Must have** |
| Dashboard with charts | High | Medium | **Must have** |
| Month-end spending forecast | High | Medium | **Should have** |
| Savings goal tracker | Medium | Low | **Should have** |
| AI suggestions / chat assistant | Medium | High | **Could have** |
| Anomaly detection | Medium | Medium | **Could have** |

## Selected Ideas for the First Release
Expense entry with auto-categorisation, category budgets with alerts, dashboard, savings goals and a spending forecast.
