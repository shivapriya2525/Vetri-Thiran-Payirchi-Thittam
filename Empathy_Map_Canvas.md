**Target user:** Young earner / college student with a monthly income or allowance, active on UPI and mobile apps.

| Section | Insights |
|---|---|
| **Says** | "I don't know where my money goes." · "I will start saving next month." · "Tracking every expense is boring." |
| **Thinks** | "Am I spending too much?" · "Will my money last till the end of the month?" · "I need a simple app that does the work for me." |
| **Does** | Pays through UPI and cards, checks bank balance often, makes notes in phone or on paper, skips logging small expenses |
| **Feels** | Anxious near month-end, guilty after impulse purchases, motivated when goals are visible, frustrated by complex apps |
| **Sees** | Friends using many finance apps, ads for offers and sales, rising prices, bank SMS alerts |
| **Hears** | "Save at least 20% of your income", advice from family, social-media money tips |
| **Pains** | Forgotten expenses, no forecasting, generic advice, overspending on food, shopping and subscriptions |
| **Gains** | Clear monthly overview, alerts before overspending, achievable savings goals, peace of mind |
