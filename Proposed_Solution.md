| S.No. | Parameter | Description |

| 1 | **Problem Statement** | People lack an easy, intelligent way to track expenses and stay within budget, leading to overspending and low savings |
| 2 | **Idea / Solution Description** | Pocket Smart AI is a web-based budget planner that logs expenses, auto-categorises them with ML, forecasts month-end spending, alerts users about overspending and suggests ways to save |
| 3 | **Novelty / Uniqueness** | Combines auto-categorisation, spending forecast and personalised tips in one lightweight app designed for pocket-sized daily use |
| 4 | **Social Impact / Customer Satisfaction** | Builds financial awareness and healthy saving habits; reduces money-related stress |
| 5 | **Business Model (Revenue)** | Free basic plan; premium plan with advanced insights, multi-goal tracking and report export |
| 6 | **Scalability of the Solution** | Modular architecture allows more categories, bank/UPI statement import, multi-currency and mobile app versions |
