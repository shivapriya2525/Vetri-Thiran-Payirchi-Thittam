## 1. Introduction
Pocket Smart AI is an AI-powered budget planner that helps users track expenses, stay within budget and save consistently.

## 2. Problem Definition
Users forget to log expenses, lack insight into spending habits and realise overspending too late. Existing apps demand heavy manual work and provide generic advice.

## 3. Objectives
- Simplify expense tracking through quick entry and auto-categorisation
- Help users set and follow category budgets
- Forecast month-end spending and warn about overspending
- Encourage savings through goals and AI tips

## 4. Literature and Existing Systems
Spreadsheets, notebooks and general finance apps exist, but they rely on manual entry and rarely offer predictive, personalised suggestions.

## 5. System Requirements
See *2. Requirement Analysis* for functional and non-functional requirements and the technology stack.

## 6. System Design
See *3. Project Design Phase* for problem-solution fit, proposed solution and architecture.

## 7. Implementation
The backend is built with Flask and SQLite. A Naive Bayes text classifier categorises expenses and a projection model forecasts month-end spending. The frontend uses HTML, CSS, JavaScript and Chart.js.

## 8. Testing
Ten functional test cases and performance checks were executed and passed. See *6. Project Testing*.

## 9. Results
The system records expenses, predicts categories, raises alerts at 80% and 100% of budget limits, and displays forecasts and charts.

## 10. Conclusion
Pocket Smart AI reduces the effort of budgeting and gives users clear, predictive insight into their money.

## 11. Future Scope
UPI and bank statement import, receipt scanning, multi-currency support, a mobile app, voice-based expense entry and a full generative-AI financial assistant.
