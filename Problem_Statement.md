## Overview
Many people in India, especially students, early-career professionals and small families, spend money through UPI, cards and cash without tracking it. By month-end they cannot explain where the money went, savings goals are missed and small daily expenses quietly drain the budget. Existing budgeting apps need heavy manual entry, give generic advice and do not adapt to individual habits.

## Problem Statement
**Pocket Smart AI** is an AI-powered budget planner that helps users record expenses easily, understand their spending patterns and receive personalised, predictive guidance so they can stay within budget and reach their savings goals.

## Problem Statement Table (Who / What / Why)
| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| **I am** | A student or working individual managing a monthly income |
| **I'm trying to** | Control spending, avoid overspending and save regularly |
| **But** | I forget to log expenses, don't know my spending pattern and only realise overspending after the month ends |
| **Because** | Manual tracking is tedious and most apps give no smart, personal suggestions |
| **Which makes me feel** | Stressed, anxious about money and out of control of my finances |

## Solution in One Line
A simple, smart pocket-sized budget assistant that categorises expenses automatically, predicts month-end spending and gives timely saving suggestions.
