## Problem-Solution Fit Canvas

| Canvas Section | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Customer Segment(s) | College students, young professionals and households who manage a monthly budget |
| Customer Constraints | Limited time, low financial knowledge, unwilling to pay for premium tools, privacy concerns |
| Available Solutions | Notebooks, spreadsheets, bank statements, general expense-tracker apps |
| Problems / Pains | Manual entry, no personalised advice, overspending, no savings plan |
| Root Cause (Why the problem exists) | Existing tools only record data and do not analyse or advise |
| Triggers to Act | Running out of money before month-end, a large upcoming expense, a bill shock |
| Emotions: Before | Stressed, guilty, confused |
| Emotions: After | Confident, in control, motivated |
| Customer Behaviour | Checks balance often, compares prices, delays saving |
| Channels (Online) | Web app, chat assistant, email or in-app alerts |
| Channels (Offline) | Word of mouth, campus and workplace awareness sessions |
| Our Solution | PocketSmart AI tracks expenses, categorises them automatically, warns about overspending and gives personalised recommendations through a conversational assistant |
